# Part III: Final Writeup - Why Are Tech Workers Leaving the U.S.?
**By Lingquan Hui**

## Project Overview
This project examines the recent surge in tech layoffs, exploring underlying causes, where displaced workers are going, and the broader implications for both the tech industry and the global job market. From the pandemic-induced hiring boom to the strategic shifts driven by AI and automation, this data story provides insights into how tech companies are reshaping their workforces and where these changes leave future tech talent.

**Shorthand Story Link:** [Why Are Tech Workers Leaving the U.S.?](https://carnegiemellon.shorthandstories.com/why-are-tech-workers-leaving-the-u-s/index.html)

---

## 1. Introduction
The tech industry is known for its rapid innovation and growth, but in 2022 and 2023, we saw unprecedented layoffs among some of the biggest names in tech. This project explores the reasons behind these layoffs, the new destinations for displaced tech talent, and what this means for tech graduates entering the job market.

**Key Questions Explored:**
- Why have tech layoffs surged recently, despite a strong U.S. economy?
- Where are former tech employees going post-layoffs?
- How is this migration influencing the global talent market?

---

## 2. Project Sections and Data Visualizations

### Section 1: Understanding the Layoffs
- **Objective:** To identify why tech companies are shedding jobs despite market resilience.
- **Key Insights:** Over-hiring during the pandemic, easy access to capital, and shifts towards AI have driven companies like Amazon and Google to reassess their workforces.
- **Visualizations:**
  - *Pandemic Hiring Boom:* Bar chart on job creation monthly from 2020 to 2023.
  - *Tech Layoffs:* Line graph showing tech layoffs by month in 2022 and 2023.
  - *Company-Specific Layoffs:* Highlighted data points, such as Twitter's 80% layoff under Elon Musk.

### Section 2: Where Are Displaced Tech Workers Going?
- **Objective:** To track where former tech employees are finding new opportunities.
- **Key Insights:** Many tech employees are moving to other industries or countries, notably Canada, which has implemented policies attracting U.S.-trained tech talent.
- **Visualizations:**
  - *Tech Layoffs by Job Category:* Bar chart showing which roles have been most affected.
  - *Canada’s Talent Market:* Heatmap of North America showing top tech talent markets.
  - *Age & Experience Breakdown:* Bar and pie charts showing demographics of displaced workers.

### Section 3: The Role of Canada and Global Talent Migration
- **Objective:** To show how Canada's tech policies are reshaping the global talent landscape.
- **Key Insights:** Canada's streamlined immigration process makes it a viable destination for displaced U.S. tech workers, impacting the U.S. talent pool.
- **Visualizations:**
  - *Top Tech Cities in Canada:* Map showing tech hubs in Canada vs. the U.S.
  - *Global Migration Trends:* Line graph comparing tech migration rates.

### Call to Action
Encouraging actionable steps:

- **For Governments & Tech Companies:**
  - *Invest in Reskilling:* Support continuous upskilling programs to help employees adapt to new tech trends.
  - *Sustainable Hiring Practices:* Avoid over-hiring and focus on strategic, long-term workforce planning.
  - *Leverage Economic Conditions:* Adjust hiring and investment strategies based on interest rates—capitalize on low rates to expand responsibly, and focus on efficiency during high rates.

- **For Students & Recent Graduates:**
  - *Focus on In-Demand Skills:* Research tech job markets and prioritize skills in areas like AI, cloud computing, and automation.
  - *Build Professional Networks:* Connect with industry professionals and mentors to gain insights and support for navigating career transitions.

## 3. Reflection on Design and Decisions

### Audience
The primary audience includes:
- Recent tech graduates
- Current tech professionals
- Policy-makers and company executives

**Focus:** A balanced perspective that informs both the workforce and leaders in the tech sector on the realities of today’s job market.

### Design Choices
- **Narrative Structure:** Designed around a chronological and thematic progression from problem to solution.
- **Data Visualizations:** Used strategically to provide visual evidence of trends discussed in the text.
- **Color Scheme:** Maintained a neutral, professional palette that reflects the seriousness of the topic while remaining visually accessible.

### Key Adjustments from Part II
Based on user research feedback, I refined the narrative to emphasize the reasons behind layoffs and included additional data on global migration trends. I also simplified the visualizations for better readability and added specific insights from Canada’s role in attracting tech talent.
